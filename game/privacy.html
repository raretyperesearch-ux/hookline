Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026 · Operated by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [JURISDICTION] · Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

We collect what the game needs to run your account and keep it fair. We don't sell your data and there are no ads.

What we collect

  • Account: an account ID, the name and coat color you pick, your level, settings, tutorial progress and Fishdex.
  • Sign-in (if you use it): your Privy user ID, email address, and your wallet address.
  • Game economy: AX balance, gear, cooler, bait, market listings and a ledger of AX changes (to keep the economy fair and fix mistakes).
  • Multiplayer: your boat's position and status while you're online, shared with other players in your room.
  • Real-money play: trades happen in your browser directly with Lighter; we may record what you caught to award in-game items.
  • Technical: IP address and basic request logs, used for security and rate limiting. If you confirm the 18+ risk acknowledgement we store when you did it.

Who else sees it

  • Other players: your name, boat, catch and round score.
  • Service providers: Privy (sign-in and wallet), Lighter (the exchange, for real trades), and our hosting provider. They process data under their own policies.
  • Legal: if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Ask us to delete your account at [CONTACT EMAIL]; we'll remove it except what we must keep by law (for example records connected to payments).

Your choices

You can play as a guest without giving us an email. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Your browser stores a session token and a copy of your save on your device; clearing site data removes them.

Children

HookLine is not directed at children under 13, and real-money play is 18+ only.
