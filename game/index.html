        How HookLine works

        Fish = a leveraged positionEvery fish that bites is a long or short on a Lighter perp market. You choose whether to reel it in.
        Rod = leverageBamboo 2× up to the Kraken Rod 50×. update_leverage
        Knife = stop lossCuts the line before a loser gets worse. create_sl_order
        Net = take profitScoops a winner into your wallet automatically. create_tp_order
        Sonar = informationSee the market and direction before you reel.
        Chest = open positionsBigger chest, more fish at once.
        AX = the shopAX buys and upgrades gear. It never buys a better trade.
        Kraken = liquidationIf a fish loses too much, the Kraken eats it.

        Cast a line. Catch a position.

        Playable mockup. Prices are simulated and sped up so things happen fast; nothing touches a chain. The trading layer is built behind an exchange adapter, so swapping the simulator for Lighter is one class.

        How to play

        1. Gear up and cast (or press Space). Every cast hooks a random long or short on one of the lake's markets. With Sonar you can tap a fish to aim at it.
        2. Green: the fish swims toward your boat. From +2% you can Pull in any time to take the profit.
        3. Red: the fish drags your boat away, red !!! pop over the fisherman and he starts yelling to cut it. Cut the line or let your knife do it.
        4. More lines: your chest decides how many casts can be out at once. Tap a slot to switch between them.
        5. Gear up with AX: rods for leverage, knives for stop losses, nets for take profit, bigger chests for more fish.

        Wired for the Lighter SDK

        Every trade goes through one Venue interface. SimVenue runs this demo; LighterVenue in the source maps each call to Lighter:

        Game actionLighter
        Prices on the tapews market_stats/all → mark_price
        Pick a rodupdate_leverage(market, ISOLATED_MARGIN_MODE, lev)
        Land a fishcreate_market_order_quote_amount(market, id, margin×lev, max_slippage, is_ask)
        Knifecreate_sl_order(…, trigger_price, reduce_only=True)
        Smart Knife trailmodify_order(…, trigger_price) as the fish grows
        Netcreate_tp_order(…, trigger_price, reduce_only=True)
        Release / cut by handcreate_market_order(…, reduce_only=True)
        Chest contentsws account_all_positions/{account}
        HookLine's cutintegrator_account_index, integrator_taker_fee

        Lighter's official signer SDKs are Python and Go, so orders are signed by a small HookLine relay (or a WASM build of the signer); prices and positions stream straight to the browser.

        Live adapter calls

        • update_leverageRod applied to the market
        • create_market_order_quote_amountA landed fish opens
        • create_sl_orderKnife set / trailed
        • create_tp_orderNet set
        • create_market_order (reduce_only)Release, cut, net or Kraken
        Real money note. Fish are random positions, so most players will lose money without stops. Knives are on by default here. Leverage products are restricted in some countries (Lighter blocks some regions), so get legal advice before launch.
