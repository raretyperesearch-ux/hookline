Risk disclosure

Leveraged trading is high risk. In HookLine, every fish you hook with real money is a real leveraged perpetual futures position on Lighter, a third-party exchange. You can lose all of the money you put on a fish (your "bait"), quickly.

What can happen

  • Leverage multiplies losses as well as gains. On a 5× rod, a 10% price move against you is a 50% loss of your bait.
  • Liquidation ("the Kraken"). If a position loses about 90% of its margin it is closed by the exchange and that margin is gone.
  • Stops and targets are not guaranteed. The knife (stop loss) and net (take profit) are real orders on Lighter. In fast markets they can fill at a worse price than shown, or not at all.
  • Prices can gap, and services can fail. Exchange, wallet, network or HookLine outages can stop you from opening or closing a position in time.
  • Fees and funding charged by the exchange (and any HookLine fee shown before you trade) reduce your returns.

It's a game, not advice

Fish, colors, sounds, sonar and voice lines are game presentation. Nothing in HookLine is investment, financial, legal or tax advice, and nothing predicts where a price will go. Only trade money you can afford to lose.

Your money and your wallet

Real-money play uses a self-custodial wallet created for you by Privy and an account on Lighter. HookLine does not hold your funds. You are responsible for your account and for any taxes on your trading.

In-game currency has no cash value

AX, fish, scraps and bait are virtual game items. They cannot be redeemed for money and are not an investment.

Where you can play for real

Real-money fishing is only for adults (18+, or the age of majority where you live) in places where trading perpetual futures is legal for you. It is not available in [RESTRICTED REGIONS]. Paper-money fishing is open to everyone.
